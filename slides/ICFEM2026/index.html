13 / Fitness

The remaining fitness factors

F(r) = clip[0,1][ A(r) G(r) P(r) + 0.06 IL(r) ]

G(r) · Breadth

1 − 0.5 h(r)/30, where h counts accepted strings among 30 auxiliary random probes.

P(r) · Structure

A mild penalty for broad or fragile constructs, such as wildcards, large classes and nested quantifiers.

IL(r) · Literals

1 if the regex contains a useful literal fragment from positive traces; 0 otherwise. The bonus is 0.06.

clip · Range

The final score stays in [0,1]. Perfect fitness meets these criteria; it does not prove equivalence with the hidden target.

The random probes participate in optimisation. They are not an independent held-out test set.
